# Testing and Implementation

## Purpose

This section describes how the proposed complaint-management solution could be tested, introduced and evaluated through a controlled implementation.

The artefacts are planning proposals. They do not represent confirmed AFCA implementation arrangements, risk ratings, service targets or governance decisions.

## Approach

The proposed implementation approach:

- validates requirements before delivery;
- introduces capabilities in controlled phases;
- retains human accountability for complaint decisions;
- includes privacy, security and accessibility throughout delivery;
- uses traceable UAT scenarios and acceptance criteria;
- assesses people, process, data and technology impacts;
- monitors implementation and operational risks; and
- evaluates success through balanced measures rather than volume alone.

## Proposed delivery sequence

1. Validate the scope, requirements and governance.
2. Establish the controlled case foundation.
3. Introduce triage and allocation support.
4. Strengthen case workflow and communication.
5. Enable operational oversight and reporting.
6. Transition into controlled continuous improvement.

Progression between phases should depend on evidence, readiness and accepted risk rather than dates alone.

## Status

All testing and implementation artefacts are **Proposed**.

Confirmed participants, dates, environments, targets, owners, risk tolerances and approval authorities require stakeholder and technical validation.
