# Requirements

## Purpose

This section translates the case-study evidence, current-state risks and future-state design into structured and traceable requirements for a proposed complaint-management solution.

The requirements are technology-neutral and do not claim to represent AFCA’s confirmed internal requirements. They require validation with authorised stakeholders before implementation.

## Requirements approach

The artefacts follow this traceability path:

**Evidence and risks → business requirements → functional and non-functional requirements → user stories → acceptance criteria**

The prioritised backlog proposes an implementation sequence, while the data dictionary defines the principal information required by the future-state process.

## Status

All requirements are currently **Proposed**.

They have not been validated or approved by AFCA or any authorised internal stakeholder. Priorities, business rules, service levels, role names, data classifications and technical constraints must be confirmed before delivery.

## Scope controls

The requirements intentionally avoid:

- inventing current internal systems or operating procedures;
- defining complaint merits or outcomes through automation;
- assuming confirmed organisational roles;
- inventing service-level targets or allocation thresholds;
- selecting a specific CRM product or technology architecture; and
- treating aggregated public data as case-level operational evidence.
