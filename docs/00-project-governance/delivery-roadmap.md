# Project Delivery Roadmap

This roadmap follows a simplified Business Analysis lifecycle. The phases are iterative, meaning new evidence may require earlier assumptions, process models or requirements to be revised.

| Phase | Focus | Planned outputs | Validation gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Project framing | Define the business question, objectives, scope and limitations | Project brief, decision log and evidence rules | The scope is achievable and does not imply internal AFCA access |
| 2. Evidence and discovery | Review public sources, acquire data and understand the documented complaint process | Source register, data inventory, glossary, stakeholder map and current-state process | Sources, definitions, data grain and limitations are documented |
| 3. Analysis | Examine complaint demand and case flow, then identify evidence-supported opportunities | Data profile, baseline analysis, pain-point register and root-cause hypotheses | Findings are reproducible and hypotheses are not presented as facts |
| 4. Future-state design | Develop the proposed process, business rules and solution requirements | Future-state process, requirements catalogue, user stories and prioritised backlog | Requirements trace to a business need, evidence item or identified risk |
| 5. Validation and delivery | Define measures, testing and implementation considerations | Dashboard, KPI dictionary, traceability matrix, UAT scenarios, risks and implementation roadmap | Recommendations are feasible, testable and supported by the available evidence |

## Delivery approach

The project will be developed incrementally. Each major decision will be recorded, factual claims will be linked to public sources, and processed data will remain traceable to an unchanged raw source.
