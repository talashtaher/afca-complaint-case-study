## Source acceptance criteria

A source can support a `FACT` only when:

- the publisher and page are identifiable;
- the information is relevant to the exact claim;
- the publication or access date is recorded where material;
- definitions and caveats are retained; and
- any calculation based on it can be reproduced.

## Notes

- AFCA's published data describes complaints and outcomes at an aggregated level. It cannot by itself prove internal process causes.
- ASIC RG 271 applies to internal dispute resolution by financial firms. It will be used as contextual evidence and will not be misrepresented as the rulebook for AFCA's own case-management process.
- Data and page contents may change. The final repository will state the analysis cut-off date.
