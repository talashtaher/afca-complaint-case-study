# Raw data

## Dataset

`afca_historical_complaints_2025_26.csv` contains monthly complaint volumes for the 2025–26 financial year.

The file includes four measures:

- complaints received
- complaints resolved during Registration and Referral (R&R)
- complaints progressed
- complaints closed

## Source

Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA) Datacube  
Historical comparison: https://data.afca.org.au/historical-comparison

Accessed: 20 September 2026

## Filters used

- Period: 1 July 2025 to 30 June 2026
- Financial firm or super fund: All members
- Primary business: All
- Business size: All
- Product group: All
- Product: All

## Collection method

The AFCA visual did not provide a downloadable CSV or table for this chart. Monthly values were therefore transcribed from the chart tooltips into a structured CSV file.

Each month was checked against its corresponding tooltip screenshot. The completed file was then validated for record count, missing values and column totals.

## Validation results

- Monthly records: 12
- Missing values: 0
- Complaints received: 117,028
- Complaints resolved in R&R: 60,442
- Complaints progressed: 58,427
- Complaints closed: 52,015

These totals were calculated from the transcribed monthly records and are used as an internal integrity check.

## Limitations

The dataset represents aggregate public information. It does not contain individual complaint records, case complexity, staff workloads, allocation decisions or internal processing times. Therefore, it can support trend analysis but cannot by itself explain the causes of operational performance.
